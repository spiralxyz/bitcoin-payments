# Contributing

This report is a living document, not a conclusion. We want it to get better as more people challenge it, add evidence and propose ideas. Anyone can contribute.

## Ways to contribute

**Join the discussion.** The easiest way to contribute is to reply in the [GitHub discussion](../../discussions), or to our posts on X, Stacker News or Delving Bitcoin. Share ideas, push back on the report or ask questions. No template needed.

**Propose a moonshot idea.** The report's central question is what could change the course of bitcoin payments. If you have an idea big enough to shift adoption, not just improve it, post it in the discussion or [open a moonshot idea issue](../../issues/new?template=moonshot-idea.yml). Ideas don't need to be finished. A clear problem, a proposed change and who could make it happen is enough to start.

**Share data.** Public data on bitcoin payments is thin. If you can share figures on payment volumes, growth, active merchants, success rates or how people pay, [open a data issue](../../issues/new?template=data.yml). Tell us what you can share publicly and what should stay private.

**Correct or challenge something.** If a claim is wrong, out of date or missing important context, [open a correction issue](../../issues/new?template=correction.yml) or send a pull request with the fix and a source.

**Improve the report directly.** For wording changes, new sources or new sections, send a pull request against `README.md`.

## Guidelines

- **Cite sources.** Link to the primary source where possible, such as an official announcement, filing or dataset, rather than coverage of it.
- **Keep it constructive.** Disagreement is welcome and expected. Argue with the ideas, not the people.
- **Be specific.** "Payments are too hard" is less useful than "receiving a Lightning payment on mobile fails when the app is closed, which affects X% of our users."
- **Stay focused on payments.** Ideas about bitcoin more broadly are better discussed elsewhere.

## Style

The report uses American spelling and lowercase "bitcoin" for both the network and the currency, except at the start of a sentence. "Lightning" is capitalized.

## How changes are made

The Spiral team reviews issues and pull requests and updates the report as we learn. Not every idea will make it into the report, but every one will be read.
